Available Optimization Algorithms
=================================

🧬 Metaheuristics Library Algorithms:
   Nature-inspired global optimization (penalty-based constraints)

   - mh:de                | Differential Evolution (robust, good convergence)
   - mh:pso               | Particle Swarm Optimization (fast exploration)
   - mh:rga               | Real-coded Genetic Algorithm (diverse search)
   - mh:tlbo              | Teaching-Learning-Based Optimization (parameter-free)
   - mh:firefly           | Firefly Algorithm (multi-modal problems)

🎵 AutoEQ Custom Algorithms:
   Specialized algorithms developed for audio filter optimization

   - autoeq:cobyla        | Constraints: ✅ Nonlinear | Pure-Rust constrained local optimization
   - autoeq:cobra         | Constraints: ✅ Nonlinear
   - autoeq:isres         | Constraints: ✅ Nonlinear | Pure-Rust stochastic constrained global optimization
   - autoeq:cmaes         | Constraints: ❌ Penalty-based | Covariance Matrix Adaptation Evolution Strategy
   - autoeq:bo            | Constraints: ❌ Penalty-based | Gaussian-process Bayesian optimization
   - autoeq:nsga2         | Constraints: ❌ Penalty-based | NSGA-II multi-objective optimization
   - autoeq:nsga3         | Constraints: ❌ Penalty-based | NSGA-III many-objective optimization
   - autoeq:de            | Constraints: ✅ Nonlinear | Adaptive Differential Evolution with constraint handling

Deprecated NLopt compatibility aliases:
   nlopt:cobyla, nlopt:slsqp, nlopt:bobyqa, nlopt:neldermead, nlopt:sbplx
      → autoeq:cobyla
   nlopt:isres, nlopt:ags, nlopt:origdirect
      → autoeq:isres
   nlopt:crs2lm, nlopt:direct, nlopt:directl, nlopt:gmlsl,
   nlopt:gmlsllds, nlopt:stogo, nlopt:stogorand
      → autoeq:de
   These names are migration aliases, not separate available backends.

Usage Examples:
==============

  # Use ISRES (recommended global optimizer):
  autoeq --algo autoeq:isres --curve input.csv

  # Use COBYLA (fast local optimizer):
  autoeq --algo autoeq:cobyla --curve input.csv

  # Use Differential Evolution from metaheuristics:
  autoeq --algo mh:de --curve input.csv

  # Short compatibility alias (maps to autoeq:cobyla):
  autoeq --algo cobyla --curve input.csv

Recommendations:
===============

  🎯 For best results: autoeq:isres (global) + --refine with autoeq:cobyla (local)
  ⚡ For speed: autoeq:cobyla (if you have a good initial guess)
  🧪 For experimentation: mh:de or mh:pso from metaheuristics library
  ⚖️  For constrained problems: Prefer algorithms with ✅ Nonlinear constraint support
